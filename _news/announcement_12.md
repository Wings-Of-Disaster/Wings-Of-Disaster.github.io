Our paper [MemVerse: Multimodal Memory for Lifelong Learning Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.03627) has been accepted by **Findings of EACL 2027**! MemVerse organizes multimodal experiences into hierarchical memories and distills essential knowledge into the model, helping agents remember, adapt, and reason over extended interactions. Code available at [Link](https://github.com/KnowledgeXLab/MemVerse). 🤗🤗
