Our paper [Mixture-of-Experts with Advanced Integrated Collaboration (Mosaic)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0950705126011676) has been accepted by **Knowledge-Based Systems (KBS)**! Mosaic uses a mixture of experts to guide data-free knowledge distillation in heterogeneous distributed environments. Congrats to all collaborators! Code available at [Link](https://github.com/Wings-Of-Disaster/Mosaic). 🎉🎉
